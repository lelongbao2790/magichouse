Grade 2 Daily Challenge

Add a Daily Challenge feature for Grade 2 students.
Each challenge contains 10 randomly selected questions from Math, Vietnamese, and English.

Students can complete the challenge once per day.
After completing all questions, calculate the score and award bonus coins based on the result:

Below 50% → 10 coins
50–79% → 20 coins
80–99% → 30 coins
100% → 50 coins

Display the student's score, earned coins, and completion status after finishing the challenge
