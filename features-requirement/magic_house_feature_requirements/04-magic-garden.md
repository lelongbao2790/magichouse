# Feature: Magic Garden

## Goal

Create long-term visual progress by letting children grow a garden
through learning.

## User Flow

1.  Buy or receive a seed.
2.  Plant the seed.
3.  Complete learning activities.
4.  The plant grows through stages.
5.  Collect flowers, fruit, or special decorations.

## Growth Example

Seed → Sprout → Small Plant → Flower

## Items

-   Seeds
-   Flower pots
-   Trees
-   Garden decorations
-   Fountain
-   Butterflies
-   Garden furniture

## Learning Connection

Each completed lesson provides growth points or water.

## Coin Economy

Coins can purchase seeds and decorative garden items.

## MVP

One garden area, 5 plant types, 3--4 growth stages, watering/growth
points, and simple decoration placement.
