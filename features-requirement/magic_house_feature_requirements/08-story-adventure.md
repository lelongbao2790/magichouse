# Feature: Story Adventure

## Goal

Improve reading and language skills through interactive stories.

## User Flow

1.  Choose a story.
2.  Read or listen to a short section.
3.  Answer a question or make a choice.
4.  Continue to the next scene.
5.  Complete the story and receive rewards.

## Learning Areas

-   English reading
-   Vietnamese reading
-   Vocabulary
-   Reading comprehension
-   Simple decision making

## Rewards

-   Coins
-   Story badges
-   Character items
-   Special stickers

## MVP

One short branching story with 5--8 scenes, simple illustrations,
comprehension questions, and completion rewards.
