# Feature: My Magic House

## Goal

Give children a personal house they can improve by learning and earning
coins.

## User Flow

1.  Complete learning activities.
2.  Earn coins.
3.  Open **My House**.
4.  Buy furniture and decorations.
5.  Drag and drop items into rooms.
6.  Save the customized house.

## Rooms

-   Bedroom
-   Study Room
-   Living Room
-   Garden
-   Pet Room

## Example Items

-   Bed --- 100 coins
-   Lamp --- 40 coins
-   Teddy Bear --- 30 coins
-   Wallpaper --- 80 coins
-   Desk --- 70 coins

## Learning Connection

Completing lessons and quizzes rewards coins that can be spent on house
items.

## Core Loop

**Learn → Earn → Buy → Decorate → Upgrade**

## MVP

Start with one Bedroom, 10--15 furniture items, drag-and-drop placement,
item ownership, and saved room layout.
