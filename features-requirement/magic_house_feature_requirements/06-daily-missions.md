# Feature: Daily Missions

## Goal

Encourage children to complete a small variety of learning activities
each day.

## Example Missions

-   Complete 1 Math activity --- 20 coins
-   Complete 1 English activity --- 20 coins
-   Answer 10 questions correctly --- reward
-   Complete all daily missions --- Magic Chest

## Magic Chest Rewards

-   Coins
-   Random sticker
-   Furniture
-   Pet food
-   Character accessory

## Design Principle

Daily missions should feel like optional bonuses. Avoid punishing
children for missing a day.

## MVP

Three daily missions, progress indicators, automatic daily reset, and
one completion chest.
