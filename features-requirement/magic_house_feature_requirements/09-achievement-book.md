# Feature: Achievement Book

## Goal

Show children their accomplishments and give them collectible goals.

## Badge Examples

-   Addition Beginner
-   Addition Master
-   100 Correct Answers
-   First English Lesson
-   Reading Explorer
-   Garden Helper
-   Pet Friend
-   House Designer

## Categories

-   Learning
-   Games
-   Collection
-   Decoration
-   Adventure

## Rewards

Some achievements can unlock coins, stickers, furniture, or special
cosmetic items.

## MVP

10--15 badges with locked/unlocked states and a simple achievement
gallery.
